FAQ

Find answers about Japan First Move, Custom Food Cards, saving images, and using the guide while traveling in Japan.

Custom Food Card

Q.

What is a Custom Food Card?

A.

A Custom Food Card helps you show allergies, dietary needs, ingredients to check, shared tools or oil, or foods you prefer to avoid in Japanese.

Q.

Is this an allergy safety card?

A.

No. This card is a communication aid. It helps you show your food needs in Japanese, but it does not guarantee food safety.

Q.

Can restaurants always follow this card?

A.

No. Some restaurants may not be able to confirm ingredients or avoid cross-contact. If they cannot help safely, they may say they cannot serve the dish.

Q.

Does this replace asking the staff?

A.

No. Please still ask the staff to confirm ingredients and preparation.

Q.

Do I need an app to use the Food Card?

A.

No. You can use it in your browser. After creating a card, you can save it as an image.

Saving images

Q.

I can’t save the image on my phone. What should I do?

A.

Press and hold the card image, then choose “Save to Photos” or a similar option. If your browser does not save it, try “Download PNG.” You can also take a screenshot and show that to staff.

Q.

Can I share the card by email or message?

A.

We recommend saving the image to your phone first, then sharing the saved image if needed. Some browsers may not share temporary preview images correctly.

About Japan First Move

Q.

What is Japan First Move?

A.

Japan First Move is a simple travel guide that helps you know what to do first in common situations in Japan.

Q.

Is this a translation app?

A.

No. It is not a live translation app. It gives short, practical steps and phrases for common travel moments.

Q.

Is this an official travel service?

A.

No. Japan First Move is an independent guide and is not affiliated with public transport, restaurants, shrines, government services, or other organizations.

Using the guide

Q.

Can I use it offline?

A.

Not fully. Please save important cards, images, or screenshots before you need them.

Q.

Is the information always up to date?

A.

We try to keep the guide useful, but rules, prices, services, opening hours, and local procedures can change. Always check local signs or ask staff when needed.
