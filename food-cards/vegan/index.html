What is included on this vegan card?

This free sample names meat, seafood, eggs, dairy, and animal-based dashi. It asks staff to check the food and point to a reply. You can open this page on your phone and show the card before ordering.

Read the ingredient list before using it. A ready-made card explains the items it names; it is not a complete list of every possible animal-derived ingredient.

Check the broth, sauces, and seasonings

Ask about ingredients used in the whole dish, including its broth and sauces. A vegetable or tofu dish may still use fish-based dashi. Removing a visible topping does not tell you what was used in the stock.

The Japanese dashi question card is useful for a separate stock question. Give staff time to check, and wait for their reply before choosing the dish.

Vegan, vegetarian, or custom?

The vegetarian sample lists meat, seafood, and animal-based dashi. This vegan sample also lists eggs and dairy. Choose the one that matches your request.
