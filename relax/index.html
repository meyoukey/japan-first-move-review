Relax in Japan

Rest, recharge, and recover during long travel days.

First moves for resting and recharging

Find places to bathe, sit, or take a quiet break.

Use an onsen or sento

Take off your shoes before entering the changing or bathing area.

Take a short break

Look for a cafe, park, bench, or rest area where sitting is allowed.

Try a Japanese toilet

Find Stop and Call first—then try the buttons.

Stay at a ryokan

Your room may turn into a bedroom while you eat.

Useful relax phrases

Quick checks before bathing, sitting, or finding a place to rest.

Are tattoos okay here?

タトゥーがあっても利用できますか？

Use before paying.

Where do I take off my shoes?

どこで靴を脱げばいいですか？

Use before entering.

Do I need a towel?

タオルは必要ですか？

Use before a bath, sauna, or changing room.

Is it okay to sit here?

ここに座っても大丈夫ですか？

Use before sitting in a public or shared space.

Can I leave my luggage here?

荷物をここに置いても大丈夫ですか？

Use at hotels, counters, or rest areas.

Is there a place to rest nearby?

近くに休憩できる場所はありますか？

Ask at a station, shop, hotel, or information counter.
