Culture in Japan

Shrines, photos, entry rules, and local manners.

First moves for respectful visits

Check entry, photos, and local rules before you act.

Visit a shrine

Pause before the torii gate and walk through calmly, slightly to the side.

Check before taking photos

Look for signs first, then ask if photos are okay.

Hear “irasshaimase”

The shop greeting sounds like a question. It isn’t.

Use “sumimasen”

“Sorry,” “excuse me,” and sometimes even “thank you.”

Useful culture phrases

Ask first, then follow the local rule.

Can I take photos?

写真を撮っても大丈夫ですか？

Use before taking photos.

Is it okay to enter here?

ここに入っても大丈夫ですか？

Use before entering.

Where should I line up?

どこに並べばいいですか？

Use when the line or waiting area is unclear.

How do I pray here?

お参りの仕方を教えてもらえますか？

Use when you want to pray respectfully.

Can I touch this?

触っても大丈夫ですか？

Use before touching objects or displays.

Should I be quiet here?

ここでは静かにした方がいいですか？

Use in temples, shrines, or quiet spaces.
