Why Did a Futon Appear While You Were at Dinner?

When you first enter a ryokan room, you may wonder where the bed is. There is tatami flooring, a low table, tea, and perhaps a lovely view—but no bed.

Then you leave for dinner and return to find futons waiting on the floor. Your room has changed genres while you were eating.

First Move

At check-in, confirm the meal and bath times. The room—and the futon—will usually follow the ryokan’s schedule.

Quick steps

  1. Confirm the meal and bath times at check-in.
  2. If the room has no bed yet, do nothing—staff may prepare the futon while you are at dinner.

Say or show this

Use these phrases to speak or point when you need help.

Use when you want to confirm the dinner time. Yuushoku wa nanji desu ka? 夕食は何時ですか？ What time is dinner?
Use when you want to check the bathing hours. Ofuro wa nanji made desu ka? お風呂は何時までですか？ Until what time is the bath open?
Use when the room system is unclear. Futon wa jibun de shikimasu ka? 布団は自分で敷きますか？ Do I lay out the futon myself?

If You See or Hear This

Oshokuji no aida ni ofuton o shikimasu. お食事の間にお布団を敷きます。 It means: We will prepare your futon while you are having dinner. Your move Leave the room normally and enjoy your meal.
Yukata wa oheya ni arimasu. 浴衣はお部屋にあります。 It means: The yukata is in your room. Your move Check the closet, drawer, or basket.
Yuushoku wa rokuji kara desu. 夕食は六時からです。 It means: Dinner starts at six. Your move Arrive at the dining room or be ready in your room at that time.

If You’re Stuck

Ryokan routines vary. Meals may be served in your room or in a dining area, and some places ask guests to prepare their own futons. Ask at check-in. This is not a test of whether you secretly understand traditional Japanese hospitality.

Tiny Culture Note

A traditional tatami room is not permanently a bedroom. During the day it can be a sitting room. At dinner it may become a private dining room. At night, the table moves and the futons come out. The bed did not arrive late—the room simply was not finished becoming a bedroom yet.
