Why Did the Cashier Give Me an Empty Coffee Cup?

You ordered coffee. You paid for coffee. The cashier handed you an empty cup.

This may feel like the shortest café scam in history, but the coffee is usually waiting at a self-service machine nearby.

First Move

Check the drink type and size on your cup, then find the matching option on the coffee machine.

Quick steps

  1. Put the cup under the nozzle.
  2. Press the option that matches the drink and size printed on the cup.
Tip

For iced coffee, some stores have cups filled with ice in a freezer. The exact system varies, so let the cup and machine labels guide you.

Say or show this

Use these phrases to speak or point when you need help.

Use while pointing to a machine option. Kono botan de ii desu ka? このボタンでいいですか？ Is this the right button?
Use at the register. Hotto koohii o hitotsu onegaishimasu. ホットコーヒーを一つお願いします。 One hot coffee, please.
Use when you have the cup but cannot find the coffee. Koohii mashin wa doko desu ka? コーヒーマシンはどこですか？ Where is the coffee machine?

If You See or Hear This

Hotto ホット It means: Hot. Your move Match this with a hot-drink cup.
Aisu アイス It means: Iced. Your move Look for a cup containing ice.
Regyuraa レギュラー / R It means: Regular size. Your move Match the letter or size shown on your cup.
Raaji ラージ / L It means: Large size. Your move Check that your cup is also marked L.
Kafe rate カフェラテ It means: Café latte. Your move Check that this is the drink you ordered.
Koime 濃いめ It means: Stronger. Your move Choose this if you want a stronger taste.

If You’re Stuck

Compare the words or symbols on the cup with the machine screen. If it is still unclear, show the cup to the cashier and point to the machine. Convenience-store coffee sometimes comes with two-step authentication: first the cashier, then the coffee machine.

Tiny Culture Note

The cashier handles the purchase, the machine handles the brewing, and you perform the final step. The empty cup is not a mistake. It is an invitation to finish making your own coffee.
