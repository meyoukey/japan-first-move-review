Why Is Your Suitcase Traveling Without You?

You leave Tokyo for Kyoto. Your suitcase leaves too—but not with you.

While you ride the train with a small bag, your suitcase follows its own itinerary and arrives at the next hotel separately. It may travel more quietly, take up less platform space, and complain about fewer stairs.

First Move

Before your travel day, ask your hotel whether it can send your suitcase to your next accommodation.

Quick steps

  1. Ask the front desk if delivery is available and when the suitcase will arrive.
  2. Show your next accommodation’s name, address, and reservation details.
  3. Keep your passport, valuables, medicine, and overnight essentials with you.
  4. Keep the receipt or tracking number.

Say or show this

Use these phrases to speak or point when you need help.

Use at the front desk before your travel day. Nimotsu o tsugi no hoteru ni okuremasu ka? 荷物を次のホテルに送れますか？ Can I send my luggage to my next hotel?
Use when you need to confirm the delivery date. Itsu todokimasu ka? いつ届きますか？ When will it arrive?
Show the destination address while saying this. Kono juusho ni onegaishimasu. この住所にお願いします。 To this address, please.

If You See or Hear This

Takuhaibin / Takkyubin 宅配便 / 宅急便 It means: Luggage or parcel delivery service. Your move Ask whether the service can deliver to your next accommodation.
Denpyou 伝票 It means: Delivery form. Your move Add the destination, sender details, and requested arrival date with staff assistance.
Motobarai 元払い It means: Paid by the sender. Your move Pay the delivery fee when sending the suitcase.
Yokujitsu 翌日 It means: The following day. Your move Prepare a small overnight bag in case your suitcase arrives tomorrow.

If You’re Stuck

Show staff the next hotel’s reservation page rather than typing the address from memory. Delivery times, accepted items, and available destinations vary. Confirm the arrival date before letting your suitcase begin its independent journey.

Tiny Culture Note

Luggage delivery turns travel into a small act of trust. Your suitcase disappears behind a hotel desk. Later, it reappears in another city as though it has learned teleportation. It has not. Japan’s delivery network is simply doing the difficult part of the trip for you.
