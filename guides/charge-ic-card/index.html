Charge an IC card

Use this when your IC card balance is low and you need to add money before riding a train or bus.

Look for チャージ or Charge on a ticket machine. If you need help, ask station staff or someone nearby.

First Move

Look for “チャージ / Charge” on a ticket machine or ask for help.

Quick steps

  1. Look for a ticket machine that says チャージ or Charge.
  2. Place or insert your IC card as shown.
  3. Choose the amount.
  4. Pay and confirm the new balance before leaving.
Tip

Many convenience stores can also charge IC cards. Ask the cashier if you are not sure.

Don’t Do This

Don’t remove your card before the machine finishes.

Wait for the updated balance and take any change or receipt.

Don’t assume every machine accepts the same payment method.

Some machines accept cash only for charging an IC card.

Say or show this

Use these phrases to speak or point when you need help.

Use when you need help charging your card. IC kaado ni chaaji shitai desu. ICカードにチャージしたいです。 I want to charge my IC card.

If You See or Hear This

Chaaji チャージ It means: Charge. Your move Choose this option to add money to your IC card.
Nyuukin 入金 It means: Add money. Your move Choose the amount you want to add.
Zandaka 残高 It means: Balance. Your move Check the updated amount before leaving.
Genkin nomi 現金のみ It means: Cash only. Your move Use cash to charge the card at this machine.
Ryoushuusho 領収書 It means: Receipt. Your move Choose this if you need a receipt.

If You’re Stuck

If you cannot find station staff, ask someone nearby or try a ticket machine with an English button. Keep the question short and show your IC card.

Tiny Culture Note

IC cards like Suica can be used on many trains and buses, but not everywhere.
